About

I am an IT manager and systems analyst with more than twenty years of experience in IT support, administration and web development. Today I lead IT at B-KELANA International, an accredited audit and accounting firm, where I design and build the internal platforms that run the firm’s archive, operations and people processes.

Alongside my career I serve the Kiyovu Sports club as Treasurer and Administration Assistant, and I lead alumni and cultural organizations. I combine software skills with policy writing, financial discipline and clear communication, and I use AI tools every day to work faster and document better.
